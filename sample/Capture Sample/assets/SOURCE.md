### Body map assets

The SVG path data is adapted from [HichamELBSI/react-native-body-highlighter](https://github.com/HichamELBSI/react-native-body-highlighter), commit 8ed39ac2ae9cb46fb79d77eedec7e5b029a75174, copyright ELABBASSI Hicham (MIT). The complete license is included in LICENSE.body-highlighter.txt.

body-paths.json contains male and female front/back views, including locally adapted hairless heads and clipped outlines. The sample renders these paths locally, without fetching remote images. Its six exercise groups combine the source’s detailed muscles: chest; shoulders; arms; core; back; and legs. Joints, neck, hands and feet remain neutral.

Copy the complete Capture Sample folder, including assets and scripts. No separate graphic import is required.
